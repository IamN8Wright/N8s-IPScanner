N8s IP Scanner v2.3.7

Reduced-Pressure Hostname release

- Cuts active discovery pressure roughly in half by limiting scanner probes to 12 devices at a time.
- Cuts hostname enrichment concurrency from six devices to three.
- Increases the post-discovery settle delay from 80 ms to 160 ms before DNS/NetBIOS hostname work begins.
- Keeps the 300 ms default probe timeout so offline addresses do not become excessively slow.
- Keeps the longer hostname-resolution budget introduced in v2.3.6.
- Intended to move closer to the hostname reliability of the older build while remaining faster than a largely sequential scan.
