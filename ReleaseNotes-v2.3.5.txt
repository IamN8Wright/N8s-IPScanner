N8s IP Scanner v2.3.5

Balanced Scan release

- Changed the default scan timeout from 200 ms to 300 ms.
- Reduced scan parallelism to avoid racing past slower AV/network endpoints.
- /24 scans now use up to 24 workers instead of 48.
- Larger scans scale to 32 or 48 workers instead of 64 or 96.
- Increased hostname-enrichment budget for slower reverse-DNS and NetBIOS responses.
- Slightly widened the web-service response window while keeping empty-address scans responsive.
- Keeps the existing InNasc XML export, OUI lookup, NIC configuration, branding, and GitHub update features.
